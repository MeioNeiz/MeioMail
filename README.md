# Meio Mail

Forever's mailbox stopped filling in the subject, so Send stays grey until you type one. Meio Mail fills it from the item or gold again, and keeps every mail you send beside the mailbox: who to, items, gold and when. Sent gold to the wrong alt, or can't remember if you paid? It's in the list.

For WoW Forever.

![Before: the mailbox's Send tab with Linen Cloth attached, an empty subject and Send greyed
out; with Meio Mail, the subject reads Linen Cloth (20), Send works, and a list of the mail
you sent sits beside it with the mouse on a row showing the boots and gold in that
mail](docs/before-after.png)

## What it does

At the mailbox's Send tab, a list of your sent mail, newest first. Handy for "did I already
send those mats?" or "how much did I give my alt?".

- Each row looks like a mailbox row: the item's icon and count, who it went to, money and
  C.O.D., and when you sent it
- Hover a row for everything in it: subject, each item and count, money
- Click a row to put that name in To again (handy for long "First Last" names)
- Prev and Next (or the mouse wheel) page back through older mail
- An empty subject fills itself from the item ("Linen Cloth (20)") or the money ("5g 25s"),
  so Send works without typing one. Your own subject always wins
- Only mail that really went out: a failed send isn't listed
- Keeps your last 50 mails for 30 days, across all your characters

Sent mail only. Works alongside Postal and its ports.

Settings: Esc > Options > AddOns > MeioMail, or `/meiomail`.

- `/sent`: the mail you sent, in chat
- `/meiomail panel on|off`: the list beside the mailbox
- `/meiomail subject on|off`: fill an empty subject from the item or money
- `/meiomail clear`: forget the sent mail

## Install

Use your addon manager (CurseForge, Wago), or unzip a GitHub release into
`World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns` and restart WoW fully.
