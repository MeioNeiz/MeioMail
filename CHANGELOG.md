# Meio Mail

  sent ("5g 25s"), so Send works without typing a subject. Off with `/meiomail subject off`

  coin icons
- Click a sent row to put that name in To again
- Prev and Next pages (and the mouse wheel) for older mail: all 50 kept mails, not just 7
